MotoTrip — Account and Data Deletion

Developer: Satish Kumar Gupta · Updated: 25 November 2026

You can delete your MotoTrip account within the app, or contact us to request account deletion and deletion of associated information. This page applies to the MotoTrip web app and Android Trusted Web Activity where the same features are available.

Option 1: Delete your account in MotoTrip

  1. Open MotoTrip and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open your Rider Profile / Profile.
  3. Choose Delete Account and follow the confirmation prompts. Re-authentication may be required.
  4. If you are an active Shared Ride member, leave that ride first if prompted.

Removing a Firebase sign-in account does not automatically delete all Shared Ride records or backups. Follow Option 2 if you also want remaining associated cloud data reviewed for deletion.

Option 2: Request account and associated data deletion

Email satish9648291@gmail.com with the subject MotoTrip Account and Data Deletion Request.

Include the email address registered to your MotoTrip account, and specify whether you want the account, your Shared Ride contributions, or both deleted. We may ask you to verify ownership before acting. Never email your password.

What data is deleted or retained?

  • Account: Deleting an account removes the Firebase Authentication account when deletion succeeds. Authentication provider records may remain temporarily according to provider requirements.
  • Local device data: Trip plans, expenses, vehicle/profile information and optional emergency details stored in your browser may remain on that device until you delete local data through MotoTrip's available profile/trip deletion controls or clear the MotoTrip site's storage. Deleting a sign-in account alone is not a guarantee that local copies are removed.
  • Shared Ride cloud data: Group names, member records, expenses, settlements and optional shared emergency cards may remain accessible to other ride members after an account is deleted. Request removal via email; some records may need to be retained in de-identified form for other members' balances or audit context.
  • Backups and third-party records: Files you exported, screenshots taken by other people, and GitHub/Firebase/map-provider service logs are not automatically removed by MotoTrip's account deletion controls.

Retention and processing

Local data is generally retained until you remove it or clear browser storage. Shared Ride records may remain while the group exists. Requests for associated cloud-data removal are reviewed individually; there is no guaranteed fixed deletion completion period for those records, and service-provider logs may have separate retention periods. We will explain any data we cannot delete and why in our response to your request.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: satish9648291@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, identification documents or sensitive trip exports unless specifically needed for a verified support request.
